## What we do

We move and deliver energy across North America: crude oil and other liquids through our pipelines, natural gas through our transmission systems and our gas utilities, and renewable power, mostly wind and solar. Our liquids network delivers roughly six million barrels a day.

## Our business units

- **Liquids Pipelines**: the Mainline System, regional oil sands pipelines, the Gulf Coast and Mid-Continent systems, export terminals and crude marketing.
- **Gas Transmission**: natural gas transmission, gathering, processing and storage in Canada and the U.S., including Texas Eastern, Algonquin and Westcoast.
- **Gas Distribution and Storage**: our rate-regulated gas utilities in Ontario, Quebec, Ohio, North Carolina, Utah, Wyoming and Idaho.
- **Renewable Power Generation**: wind and solar farms, mostly, in North America and Europe.

People often say **BU** for business unit.

## Terms people use

- **Mainline**: the Canadian Mainline and the Lakehead System (its U.S. part), which carry crude oil and other liquids from western Canada into the U.S. The Canadian Mainline can move about 3.2 million barrels a day.
- **Line 5**: our dual crude oil and liquids pipelines, including the crossing of the Straits of Mackinac.
- **Texas Eastern** and **Algonquin**: our U.S. gas transmission systems. Texas Eastern runs from the Gulf Coast to Ohio, Pennsylvania, New Jersey and New York; Algonquin continues from New Jersey into New England.
- **Westcoast**: our BC Pipeline system in British Columbia.
- **Enbridge Gas Ohio**, **Enbridge Gas Utah** and **Enbridge Gas North Carolina**: the gas utilities we bought from Dominion Energy in 2024 (formerly East Ohio Gas, Questar Gas and PSNC).
- **Wexpro**: gas supply for the Utah, Wyoming and Idaho utilities.
